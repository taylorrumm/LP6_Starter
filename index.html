Wisconsin Cheese Festival

Join us for a full weekend celebrating Wisconsin's proud cheesemaking heritage. Sample award-winning cheeses from over 40 local creameries, watch live cheese-making demonstrations, and enjoy food, music, and family activities all weekend long.

About the Festival

The Wisconsin Cheese Festival brings together the state's best creameries, cheesemakers, and food artisans for one unforgettable weekend. Now in its twelfth year, the festival has grown from a small county gathering into one of the Midwest's largest celebrations of dairy craftsmanship.

Whether you're a lifelong cheese enthusiast or just curious about how your favorite cheddar is made, there's something here for everyone. Guided tastings, cheesemaker meet-and-greets, and hands-on demonstrations run throughout each day.

What to Expect

Expect over 40 creamery booths, live cheese-curd-making demonstrations every hour, a kids' activity tent, and food trucks featuring cheese-forward dishes from around the state. Parking and shuttle service are available at the fairgrounds.

Festival Schedule

The festival runs Friday evening through Sunday afternoon. Below is a general overview of each day — a detailed hour-by-hour schedule will be emailed to registered attendees one week before the event.

  • Friday: Opening reception and evening tastings, 5:00 PM - 9:00 PM
  • Saturday: Full festival day, creamery booths and demonstrations, 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM
  • Sunday: Family day, kids' activities and closing tastings, 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM

Cheesemaker Demonstrations

Live demonstrations are held every hour on the main stage. Featured cheesemakers this year include creameries from Green County, Door County, and the Fox Valley region.
